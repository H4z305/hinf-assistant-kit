# HINF Degree Roadmap

**Program:** Bachelor of Applied Health Science in Health Informatics (HINF),
Qassim University, College of Public Health and Health Informatics.
**Plan:** 134 credit hours, 8 levels, plan version 341.

> Personal progress — your current level, term, GPA, elective picks, graduation
> target — is not in this file. Onboarding writes those into your `CLAUDE.md`.

CSC105 Computer Skills · PHYS110 Physics 1 · PSYCH101 Thinking & Learning Skills · STAT101 Statistics

## Level 2
